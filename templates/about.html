ABOUT PHISHGUARD

What is phishing?

Phishing tricks people into revealing credentials or payment data via look-alike websites, usually delivered by links.

How phishing URLs work

Attackers use long or deceptive domains, extra subdomains, brand names, IP hosts, unusual TLDs and urgency keywords to appear legitimate.

How machine learning helps

A classifier learns statistical patterns from lexical and structural URL features, generalising to URLs not seen before.

How PhishGuard analyses URLs

URL string → parse → 30 features → ML probability → rule-based indicators → combined risk score (0.6·ML + 0.4·heuristics) → feature-based explanation. The URL is never visited.

Limitations

Lexical analysis cannot see page content, domain age or reputation. Legitimate but unusual URLs may be flagged, and well-crafted phishing URLs may be missed.

Responsible use

This is a defensive, educational tool. Never treat any result as a guarantee of safety.
